AI Feature Extraction from Drone Orthophotos

Development & Optimization of an AI Model — independent research project

High-resolution drone orthophotos carry huge amounts of geospatial detail — but pulling meaningful features out of them by hand doesn't scale. This project builds an AI-assisted workflow that automates that identification and extraction, aiming at faster, more consistent results than manual interpretation.

I designed and built the CAD model for the capture drone, then worked through the image processing and AI-assisted extraction pipeline end to end — from problem definition to evaluating how well the outputs held up against manual analysis.

Where it's aimed

  • Digital soil mapping and land-use planning
  • Environmental monitoring at field or site scale
  • Precision agriculture
  • Engineering and geospatial analysis workflows

What I actually did

  • Defined objectives and designed the end-to-end workflow
  • Studied and applied image processing / computer vision techniques
  • Integrated AI-assisted feature extraction methods
  • Generated georeferenced outputs for spatial visualization
  • Evaluated accuracy and efficiency against manual interpretation
